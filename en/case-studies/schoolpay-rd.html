SchoolPay RD: a proprietary product solving school billing management

Transparency note: SchoolPay RD is a proprietary product in the demo/prototype stage, not a production platform with schools paying as customers today. I include it in the portfolio because it shows how I design and structure a complete SaaS product from scratch, including the interface, the user flow, and functional demo credentials.

The problem it targets

Many private schools, daycare centers, and tutoring centers in the Dominican Republic manage tuition, delinquency, and enrollment in spreadsheets or separate notebooks, with no centralized visibility or an easy way to track overdue payments.

The product proposal

A single dashboard where a school administrator can manage tuition, delinquency, enrollment, transportation, and announcements — with notifications sent to parents via WhatsApp instead of relying solely on paper notices.

Features designed

  • Tuition and delinquency tracking from a central dashboard
  • Enrollment and school transportation management
  • Announcements to parents via WhatsApp
  • Print-ready receipts and reports
  • Admin access with functional demo credentials

Tech stack

React + Vite + Tailwind CSS 4, with React Router for navigation. It's a fully functional interface running on demo data — the natural next step for this product would be connecting a real backend (persistent authentication, a payments database, a payment gateway) based on a real school's specific needs.
