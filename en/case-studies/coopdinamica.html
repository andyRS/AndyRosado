CoopDinámica: modernizing a financial cooperative's digital presence

The context

CoopDinámica is a Dominican financial cooperative. Like any financial institution, its website has to convey two things at once that usually compete with each other: institutional trust and a modern experience that doesn't feel dated next to today's digital banking.

The challenge

Financial institution sites tend to fall into one of two extremes: they look so "corporate" they feel a decade old, or they modernize so much they lose the seriousness a client expects from an entity handling their money. On top of that, a cooperative usually has many distinct services (loans, savings, insurance) that need to be organized without overwhelming the user.

The solution

A React site with a modern visual language — glassmorphism, dark mode, and Framer Motion animations — without sacrificing institutional seriousness: the navigation uses a mega menu that organizes all of the cooperative's services into clear categories, instead of an endless flat menu.

Key features

  • Glassmorphism and dark mode design for a modern image
  • Mega menu navigation to organize multiple financial services
  • Interface animations with Framer Motion
  • Deployed on Vercel

Tech stack

React · Framer Motion · Vercel
