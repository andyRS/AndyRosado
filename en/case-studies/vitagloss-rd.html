VitaGloss RD: e-commerce and sales management for an independent distribution business

The context

Independent Amway distributors in the Dominican Republic sell personal care and health products, but usually operate without a platform of their own — relying on PDF catalogs, WhatsApp, and spreadsheets to track orders and customers.

The challenge

Build a real online store (not just a static catalog) that also worked as a sales management system: one where the distributor could manage products, view orders, and have a panel separate from the public site their customers see.

The solution

A full-stack platform with a React 19 frontend (Vite, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, React Router) and a Node.js/Express backend, Mongoose over MongoDB Atlas, and JWT authentication with passwords hashed via bcryptjs — the standard technical foundation for an admin panel that handles customer and sales data securely.

Key features

  • Product catalog with conversion-focused presentation
  • Authentication system for the distributor's admin panel
  • Sales management separate from the store's public view
  • Responsive design with Framer Motion animations

Tech stack

React 19 · Vite · Tailwind CSS · Framer Motion · React Router v7 · Node.js · Express · MongoDB Atlas (Mongoose) · JWT · bcryptjs
