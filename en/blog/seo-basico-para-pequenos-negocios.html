Basic SEO for Small Businesses: 7 Steps to Show Up on Google

SEO sounds technical and complicated, but at its core it's simple: it's about helping Google understand what your business does and who it's for, so it shows you to the right people when they search for something related. You don't need to be a digital marketing expert to take the first steps. Here are 7 concrete actions you can start applying today.

1. Claim your Google Business profile

It's free and probably the most important thing you can do. Go to Google Business Profile, create or claim your business, and fill out all the information: address, hours, phone number, business category, and real photos. This is what gets you to show up on Google Maps and in local results when someone searches "near me."

2. Use the words your customers actually search for

Think like your customer, not like yourself. If you sell furniture in Santiago, your customers probably search "furniture Santiago" or "furniture store in Santiago," not generic terms like "quality furnishings." Include those phrases naturally in your website's titles and copy.

3. Have clear titles and descriptions on every page

Every page on your site should have a unique title that describes exactly what it's about (the "title tag") and a short description (the "meta description") that invites a click. This is what people see directly in Google's results, before they ever visit your site.

4. Optimize load speed

Google penalizes slow sites, and so do users: most people abandon a page that takes more than 3 seconds to load. Heavy images are the most common cause — compressing them and using modern formats like WebP can make a huge difference without losing visual quality.

5. Make sure your site works well on mobile

More than half of web traffic in the Dominican Republic comes from phones. If your site doesn't look or work well on a small screen, Google notices and ranks you lower — on top of losing customers directly.

6. Get real reviews from your customers

Google reviews don't just build trust with new customers, they're also a signal Google uses to decide how relevant and trustworthy your business is. Ask your satisfied customers to leave a review — most will gladly do it if you ask them directly.

7. Publish useful content consistently

A blog with articles related to your industry (like this one) gives Google more content to index and more chances for you to show up in different searches. You don't need to publish every day — with 1 or 2 well-written articles a month, focused on answering your customers' real questions, you're already ahead of most of your local competition.

SEO doesn't produce results overnight, but it's one of the investments that pays off the most in the long run: unlike paid advertising, the traffic you earn through SEO keeps coming even after you stop actively investing in it.
