Why Your Business Needs a Website (Even If You Already Have Instagram)

"I already have my Instagram, why do I need a website?" — it's one of the most common questions I get from business owners in Santo Domingo and the rest of the country. And it makes sense: Instagram and Facebook are free, easy to use, and your customers are already there. But relying only on social media has hidden costs that most people don't notice until they've already lost sales because of it.

You don't own your own space

When you post on Instagram or Facebook, you're building your business inside a platform you don't control. If the account goes down, gets hacked, or the algorithm decides to show your posts to fewer people, there's no way to get that back. A website is yours: no one can take it away from you or change the rules overnight.

You lose customers searching on Google, not Instagram

Many people, before buying or hiring a service, search Google for the business name or the type of service they need ("plumber in Santiago", "bakery in Santo Domingo"). If you don't have a website, those potential customers simply can't find you — they go straight to the competitor who does show up in the results.

It builds more trust than a social profile

A well-kept Instagram profile helps, but a professional website communicates a different level of seriousness. It shows your business is stable, that you've been around for a while, and that you invest in a good experience. For higher-value services (construction, health, consulting, technology), that extra trust is often the difference between someone reaching out or just scrolling past.

You can sell and capture leads 24/7, without depending on an algorithm

On social media, your reach depends on how many people see your post in the next few hours — after that, it gets lost in the scroll. A website keeps working for you every day, showing up on Google, receiving contact forms and WhatsApp messages while you sleep.

The ideal combination: social media + a website

It's not about choosing one or the other. Social media is great for showing day-to-day content and connecting directly with your audience. Your website is the solid foundation where those followers turn into real customers: that's where they find your complete catalog, your pricing, your contact form, and everything they need to decide.
