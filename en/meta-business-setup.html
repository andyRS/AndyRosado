Service boundaries

Setup, not monthly ad management

Ad creation, copy, creative, targeting, and campaign optimization are quoted separately.

No artificial promises

Meta controls approvals and restrictions. Technical setup does not guarantee sales, ROAS, or account and product approval.

Ad spend is separate

Advertising budget, Meta charges, external tools, and advanced corrections are excluded unless agreed in writing.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to share my passwords?

No. Add me as a person or partner with the minimum required permissions. Your company retains ownership of the assets.

Can you install the pixel on any website?

It depends on the platform and available access. Before quoting, I confirm whether the integration is direct, through a plugin, Google Tag Manager, or additional development.

Does this include campaigns and ads?

Not by default. This service prepares the infrastructure. Campaign creation and management can be added as a separate scope.

Can you recover a restricted account?

I can diagnose the case and support the process, but resolution depends on Meta and requires a specific quote.

Are your Meta assets scattered or incomplete?

I review the current state and propose only the setup your business actually needs.
