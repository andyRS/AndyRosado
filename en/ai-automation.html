Automations designed around your operation

This isn't about installing a generic bot. First, I identify where work repeats itself, which decisions need to keep human oversight, and which systems need to exchange information. Then I build a measurable, documented workflow that's prepared to handle errors.

What I can connect

  • WhatsApp and other support channels with agents specialized by product or service
  • CRM, forms, databases, email, and internal tools
  • Contact classification, campaign attribution, and lead distribution
  • Hand-off between automation and your human team, with full conversation context
  • Alerts, reports, and fallback protocols for when an external service fails

AI assists; your team keeps control

Every implementation defines clear boundaries: when the automation responds, when it should stop, and how a person steps in. The goal is to increase your business's capacity without hiding risks or relying on improvised responses.
