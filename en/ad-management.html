What the management includes

This service assumes an ad account and Meta assets already set up. If yours aren't organized yet, that work is quoted as Meta Business Suite setup.

Ad creation and duplication

New variants and tests, evaluating in each case whether to duplicate (to avoid resetting a live ad's learning phase) or edit directly.

Budget control

Pausing and reallocation within shared ad sets, taking advantage of Meta's automatic budget consolidation.

Destination URL verification

Character-by-character review of tracking URLs before pausing any ad with real spend, to avoid losing attribution data.

Custom tracking pipeline

Custom API routes that capture the fbclid and redirect to the corresponding form, with cron jobs syncing Meta Ads Insights daily.

Reports with real metrics

Impressions, clicks, CTR, spend, leads, and cost per lead, calculated from synced data — not estimates.

Ongoing monitoring

Periodic review of each ad's performance to decide what to scale, pause, or adjust based on real results.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to already have my ad account set up?

Yes. This service manages campaigns on an already active account. If you need it set up from scratch, that scope is quoted as Meta Business Suite setup.

Does it include a custom results panel?

Not always by default, but it can be added when the project needs individual per-lead attribution instead of just Meta Ads Manager's aggregate numbers.

How do you avoid losing the performance of an ad that already works?

By evaluating in each case whether to duplicate (to avoid resetting the learning phase) or edit directly, and verifying any tracking URL before pausing an ad with real spend.

Do you work with small budgets?

Yes. The Club Esperanza case, for example, was managed with a reduced daily budget during its pre-event acquisition phase.

Do your Meta Ads campaigns need better tracking?

I review your current account and propose the management scope you actually need.
